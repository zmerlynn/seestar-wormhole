Welcome to seestar-copy

Copy your Seestar's images to this computer. You need two things: where to find the telescope, and a folder for the images.

seestar-copy

Your Seestar's IP address on this network, such as 192.0.2.10, or seestar-wormhole's address, such as 127.0.0.1:4445.

Images are copied here, in the same folders as on the telescope.seestar-copy remembers what it copied per folder. A new folder starts a new history: everything on the telescope will be copied there, and files already there are reused.

One pattern per line. A name like Video matches at any depth; one starting with / is a path from the top of the share.

Leave empty to copy everything not left out.

You can change these later in Settings.
